A bacon cheese burger recipe

Ingredients

  • Ground beef
  • Bacon(thick-cut slices)
  • Cheese(American or sharp cheddar)
  • Burger buns(Brioche or potato buns)
  • Unsalted butter
  • Seasonings(salt & black pepper)
  • Toppings(lettuce,tomato slice,red onion,pickles)
  • Sauce(Mayonnaise,Ketchup,or mustard)

Steps

  1. Cook bacon: Fry bacon in a skillet until crispy. Drain on paper towels
  2. Toast buns: Spread butter on buns. Sear face-down in skillet until golden
  3. Shape patties: Form beef into patties. Make them wider than the bun
  4. Sear beef: Heat skillet to high. Season patties generously with salt and pepper
  5. Flip patties: Cook 3 minutes until a crust forms. Flip the patty
  6. Melt cheese: Immediately add cheese and bacon on top. Cover to melt
  7. Assemble burger: Spread sauce on bottom bun. Add lettuce, tomato, and onion
  8. Serve hot: Place the cheesy bacon patty on top. Close with top bun
