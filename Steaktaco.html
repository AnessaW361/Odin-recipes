A Steak Taco recipe

Ingredients

  • Steak(Flank,skirt, or flap meat)
  • Marinade(Lime juice,olive oil,garlic,cumin,chili powder)
  • Tortillas (Small corn or flour tortillas)
  • Toppings (Finely diced white onion, fresh cilantro, sliced radishes)
  • Acid/Heat (Lime wedges and your favorite salsa)

Steps

  1. Marinate steak: Whisk citrus juices, oil, and spices. Marinate beef for 1 to 4 hours
  2. Prep toppings: Finely dice the white onion and chop the fresh cilantro. Mix together
  3. Heat pan: Get a heavy cast-iron skillet or grill smoking hot over high heat
  4. Sear steak: Remove meat from marinade. Sear for 3–5 minutes per side until charred
  5. Rest meat: Transfer steak to a board. Let rest for 5 minutes before cutting
  6. Warm tortillas: Heat tortillas on a dry skillet until soft and pliable
  7. Slice beef: Slice the steak against the grain into small, bite-sized cubes
  8. Assemble tacos: Pile meat into tortillas. Top with onion-cilantro mix, salsa, and lime
