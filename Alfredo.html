A delicious alfredo recipe

Ingredients

  • Fettuccine Pasta
  • Heavy Cream
  • Unsalted Butter
  • Parmigiano-Reggiano Cheese
  • Garlic
  • Salt & black pepper
  • Fresh Parsely

Steps

  1. Boil pasta: Cook fettuccine in salted water until al dente
  2. Melt butter: Melt 1/2 cup butter in a large skillet over medium heat
  3. Saute garlic: Add 2 minced cloves. Cook for 1 minute until fragrant
  4. Simmer cream: Pour in 2 cups heavy cream. Simmer for 5 minutes
  5. Add cheese: Turn off heat. Stir in 1.5 cups grated parmigiano-Reggiano
  6. Toss noodles: Drain pasta,add to skillet,and toss until coated
  7. Season: Season with salt and black pepper. Garnish with fresh parsely
